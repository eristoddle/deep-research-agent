# Non-technical families (health, law and policy, finance, history)

**Parked** 2026-08-21 — attach when a project actually needs one, per PLAN.md D2.

Nothing in the routing needs to change to make room for them. A new family is **one row in `ROUTING.md` with its own yes/no discriminating question**, exactly like the existing ones.

`competitor-content` is the worked example and the proof the design generalizes: it was the first family question that is not about technology at all, and adding it required no change to any existing family.

The reason to wait is D2 — a family invented before a real question needs it is a guess about what sources hold the answers, and guessed sources route the agent to plausible sites that turn out to be empty. Health and law in particular have strong authoritative-source hierarchies (regulators, primary legislation, peer-reviewed guidance) that are worth discovering properly via `/research-add-module` rather than assuming.
